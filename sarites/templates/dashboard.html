Quick Entry (NLP)

Type a natural-language command in the main input box and press Enter or click Add.

  • 5 Coke — sell 5 Coke (defaults to Sale)
  • +10 Rice — stock in 10 Rice
  • -3 Sardines — stock out / remove 3 Sardines
  • 2 Coffee @Juan — sell 2 Coffee on credit to Juan

Auto-complete will suggest matching items as you type.

Items

Manage your product catalog in the Items tab or at the Items page.

  • Each item has a name, price, and quantity on hand.
  • Add/edit/delete items via the modal forms.
  • Use Upload Excel to bulk import from a spreadsheet with columns: name, price, qty.
  • Stock levels are color-coded: In Stock Low Out

Transactions

Every stock movement is recorded as a transaction. Filter by Today, This Week, This Month, or a custom date range.

Unpaid credit sales show a Credit badge. Click Mark Paid to convert them to regular sales.

Creditors

Track customers who buy on credit. When you use @Name in the quick entry, the transaction is automatically linked to that creditor.

  • Click a creditor's name to see their detail page with all unpaid transactions.
  • Use Mark Paid on individual items or Mark All Paid to clear everything.
  • Use the search bar to filter the creditor list.

Analytics

Four charts updated in real-time based on your selected period (7/30/90 days):

  • Daily Sales Trend — line chart of sales over time
  • Transaction Type Breakdown — doughnut chart of stock in/out, sales, and credit
  • Top Selling Items — horizontal bar chart of highest revenue items
  • Creditor Balances — bar chart of outstanding debts
